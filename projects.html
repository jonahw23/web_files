I love making things to solve problems. When you look at neat problems, you find cool solutions.

FLIComet

Independently designed and developed a modern C++ camera-control application for FLI's Aurora CMOS cameras, consolidating decades of legacy software into a streamlined interface. Integrates directly with camera hardware to provide precise control and support for global production use.

Starships 'n Systems

Created a science fiction one-shot RPG that incorporates CTF-style algorithmic and networking coding challenges. Built to test my friends' cybersecurity and DSA problem-solving in a high-stress (but fun) environment.

Dreamcatcher

Co-founded and developed an AI-powered social dream journal that helps users record, share, and explore their dreams with friends. Built a cross-platform mobile app with a Go backend and PostgreSQL, incorporating semantic embeddings to surface connections between dreams.

Munch

Designed and built a community-driven app that helps RIT students find free food on campus. Developed with Firebase, Munch lets users report food events and help others discover available food nearby.

qDungeon

Co-developed an in-browser, turn-based dungeon crawler inspired by NetHack and classic roguelikes. Built with React for responsiveness, the project provided hands-on experience creating dynamic web applications and interactive game mechanics.

NetHack RL Project

Joined a University of Maryland-affiliated research team in summer 2022 to explore reinforcement learning techniques for the NetHack Learning Environment. Gained early research experience applying AI to the challenge of training agents in a complex, procedurally generated game.
